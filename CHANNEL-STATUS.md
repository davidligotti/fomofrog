## Verification protocol

1. The owner confirms the exact account or invite from an authenticated channel.
2. The account publishes the canonical mint and website, and the website links back to the same account.
3. Admin/ownership control is checked without requesting passwords, private keys, or recovery secrets.
4. The verified URL is added to GitHub metadata and the website identity registry.
5. CI and a public read-back confirm the final link; conflicting aliases remain labeled retired or unverified.

No channel should publish price promises, guaranteed returns, fake metrics, fabricated listings, or instructions to send funds to an unverified address.
